THE WHIP!

Roll call is coming. You have 15 moves to find the wavering legislators on the Capitol floor and lock down their votes. Then teach a Q-learning agent to do it for you.

1 · The game

Moves left15
Votes0 / 3
Score0
Wavering (visit to secure) Secured Loyal (nothing happens) 🏃 You, the whip

Use the arrow keys or the buttons. You win by securing 3 of the 4 votes before roll call.

2 · The points (reward)

Face timeevery time the whip lands on ANY member, even repeats
Every movetime is short, so each step costs a little
Secure a wavering votefirst visit to a wavering member
Visit a loyal memberthey were already with you
Bump into the walltrying to leave the map
Goal bonusthe 3rd vote is secured

Changing a number resets the agent and your current game, because what it learned was for the old points.

3 · The Q-learning agent

Settings: learning rate α = 0.2, discount γ = 0.95, Q-values start at 0, ties broken at random, fixed random seed (so the same clicks give the same results). "Watch it play" never explores.

4 · Learning curve

Average reward per 50 training games (left axis) Share of games reaching the real goal: 3 of 4 votes (right axis)

The reward line depends on the points you set above. The goal line does not: it counts only whether the whip really got 3 votes. When the two lines disagree, the points are rewarding the wrong thing.
